1. Your Right to Request Deletion

FirstReach respects your privacy and provides a way to request deletion of personal information processed in connection with our WhatsApp communication and independent academic support services.

You may request deletion of your information even if you no longer use our services.

2. How to Submit a Deletion Request

To request deletion of your personal information, send an email to:

Use the subject line: FirstReach Data Deletion Request.

Include enough information for us to locate the relevant records, such as the WhatsApp number associated with your communication.

Please do not send passwords, identification documents, or other sensitive information unless specifically necessary and requested through a secure process.

3. Verification of Requests

To protect personal information, we may take reasonable steps to verify that a deletion request comes from the person whose data is involved.

We will request only information reasonably necessary to verify the request.

4. Information Covered

Depending on the records held, a deletion request may concern:

  • WhatsApp phone numbers and contact names.
  • Stored WhatsApp message content.
  • University or course information.
  • Communication and service enquiries.
  • Delivery and response records.
  • Other associated personal information.

5. How Requests Are Handled

We will review the request, verify identity where necessary, and assess which records can be deleted.

We will respond within the time required by applicable data-protection law and explain any applicable exceptions or additional information needed.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate record-keeping obligations, or to maintain a minimal suppression record so that an opt-out preference is respected.

6. Stop Future Marketing Messages

If you want to stop receiving marketing messages without requesting deletion of all information, you can reply STOP in the WhatsApp conversation or contact us using the email address above.

We will record and respect applicable communication opt-out requests.

7. Third-Party Services

WhatsApp communications also involve services operated by Meta. Some information may be subject to Meta's independent data-handling policies.

A deletion request to FirstReach does not automatically remove information independently held by WhatsApp, Meta, or other organisations.
